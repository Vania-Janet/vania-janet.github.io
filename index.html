Data Science · Natural Language Processing

Vania Janet Raya Ríos

AI Researcher · UNAM / IIMAS · Mexico

I am a Data Science researcher at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM). My work focuses on neural information retrieval, evidence-grounded language models, and efficient AI systems. I have conducted research at IIMAS–UNAM, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), and the University of Zurich.

My projects span multi-turn retrieval-augmented generation, large language model compression for machine translation, and clinical NLP for phenotype normalization. I am interested in the reliability, efficiency, and accessibility of AI systems, and I am an HKPFS applicant for 2027/28.

2026 / WMT

Tiny Titans at WMT26: Task-Calibrated Quantization and Activation-Aware Low-Rank Compression of Gemma 3 12B

Vania Raya-Rios*, Aleksandr Klimchuk*, Nicolas M. Cuadrado, Ivo Gollini Navarrete, Samuel Horvath, Martin Takac. * Equal contribution.

Proceedings of the Eleventh Conference on Machine Translation (WMT 2026), pp. 2037–2044.

Task-calibrated 4-bit GPTQ retained 99.7% of the dense model’s development-set COMET score while reducing GPU memory from 26.6 to 17.6 GiB. Also studied activation-aware low-rank compression and its interaction with quantization.

2026 / SemEval

IIMAS-RAG at SemEval-2026 Task 8: Hybrid Sparse-Dense Retrieval and Answerability-Conditioned Generation for Multi-Turn RAG

Vania Raya-Rios, Helena Gómez-Adorno, Leon Hecht, Pedro Vázquez-Osorio, Erick Fabián-Sandoval, Jesús Vázquez-Osorio, Diego Hernández-Bustamante.

Proceedings of the 20th International Workshop on Semantic Evaluation (SemEval 2026), pp. 2744–2753.

A modular multi-turn RAG system combining query rewriting, SPLADE and Voyage dense–sparse retrieval, reciprocal rank fusion, and answerability-aware generation. Ranked 4th of 38 in retrieval (nDCG@5 = 0.5445); recognized with the Best System Paper Award.
