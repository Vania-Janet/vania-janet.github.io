## About Me
I am a Data Science graduate from UNAM (IIMAS) specializing in **efficient, ethical, and risk-aware artificial intelligence systems**. My research focuses on model optimization and understanding where compressed neural networks break under operational stress. 

I am currently an applicant for the **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) 2027/28**, aiming to join Dr. Xingyi Yang's group at PolyU. My upcoming doctoral research plans to bridge network optimization with generative world models, creating mathematical bounds to quantify rollout trust and error propagation over long sequences.
